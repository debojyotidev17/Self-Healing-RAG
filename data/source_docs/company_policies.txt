AcroCorp Employee Handbook & Company Policies

1. Working Hours & Core Schedule
AcroCorp operates on a hybrid and remote-first model. All full-time employees are required to be online and available during core operating hours, which are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST), Monday through Friday. Flexible scheduling outside core hours is permitted subject to team lead approval.

2. Paid Time Off (PTO) Allocation
Full-time employees receive 20 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) annually, accrued on a monthly basis. In addition, AcroCorp observes 11 standard company holidays per calendar year. Unused PTO can carry over into the next calendar year up to a maximum limit of 5 days; any excess accrued PTO above 5 days is forfeited on December 31st.

3. Equipment Stipend & Technology Expense
New full-time hires receive a one-time home office technology stipend of $1,500 USD to purchase ergonomic desks, monitors, keyboards, or accessories. Laptops are provided directly by AcroCorp IT Operations. Laptops must be replaced or refreshed every 3 years.

4. Travel & Meal Reimbursement Limits
When traveling for business purposes, employees are eligible for daily meal expense reimbursements up to a maximum of $75 USD per day. Receipts must be submitted through the AcroCorp Expensify portal within 14 calendar days of returning from business travel. Alcoholic beverages are excluded from company reimbursement unless part of an authorized client dinner.

5. Learning & Professional Development
AcroCorp provides every employee with an annual learning budget of $1,000 USD for technical certifications, conference tickets, and educational books.
